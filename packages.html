Scope & Service Terms

Important details about package coverage and service responsibilities.

01

Bridge includes one commercial payer application and one eligible individual Medicare or Medicaid application. The government application is one program only; additional government applications are quoted separately.

02

Expand and Complete cover commercial payer applications. Medicare, Medicaid, other government programs, and organizational enrollment are not included in those package prices unless listed in a written quote.

03

Commercial payer applications may cover applicable lines of business, subject to the payer’s offerings and the provider’s eligibility. Confirm payer selection and line of business at intake.

04

One payer application means one provider submitted to one payer network. A payer network may offer more than one line of business; one application does not mean enrollment in every line or product.

05

Packages cover one individual provider. Group enrollment, multiple locations, tax IDs, ownership changes, facility enrollment, and complex state-specific application paths require a separate quote.

06

Package prices are ValidWRK service fees. Payer, government, licensing, DEA, and other third-party fees are not included, if applicable.

07

Providers supply accurate information and current documents, sign required forms, respond to requests, and personally confirm profile accuracy and complete any required attestation.

08

ValidWRK prepares and tracks applications but cannot guarantee payer acceptance, contract terms, effective dates, or processing times. Follow-up is included through a decision for a complete application; appeals, restarts, and material corrections may be quoted separately.

09

All specialties and provider types may be considered. Eligibility and application requirements are confirmed during intake.
