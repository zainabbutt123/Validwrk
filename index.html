The way we work

THE
VALID
APPROACH

Our work is guided by five principles that define how we serve providers: accuracy, accountability, ethical practices, and a commitment to results.

V

Verification

We ensure licenses, education, training, certifications, and required credentials are current and valid.

A

Accuracy

We prepare error-free applications, submit them to the right payers on time, and follow up consistently.

L

Link

We connect providers to the right payers, networks, and resources to help them get enrolled and start seeing patients.

I

Integrity

We follow applicable guidelines, protect provider information, and conduct every step of the process ethically and honestly.

D

Dedicated

We listen to our providers, understand their needs, and are committed to getting enrollment right the first time and helping them reach in-network status.
