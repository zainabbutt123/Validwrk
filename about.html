How credentialing works

A clear process. No guessing what comes next.

Four stages, with the services and follow-up defined around your selected package.

01

Review

We review your documents and provide verification support for licenses, DEA, board certification, and malpractice coverage, as applicable.

02

Prepare

We help with NPI and CAQH setup and organize your initial credentialing packet, flagging missing or incomplete information.

03

Submit

For enrollment services, we prepare and support submission to the commercial, Medicare, or Medicaid payers included in your scope.

04

Follow Up

We track progress, communicate with payers, and share status updates throughout the agreed service period. Payers make the final decisions.
