NPI Record Guide

Your NPI Record

A practical guide to reviewing your details in the free NPI Public Registry.

Your NPI helps identify who you are, what you do, and where you practice.

Accurate information supports Credentialing and provider enrollment.
Step 1 • Start Here

Review the Public Directory

Visit npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov/search to use the free NPI Registry.

Search by your NPI number or provider name. Review the record as it appears today, before making changes.

The public directory is for reviewing information. You do not need to log in to search.
NPI Types

Type 1 vs. Type 2

NPI Type 1 is for an individual provider, such as an MD, DO, NP, PA, RN, or therapist.

NPI Type 2 is for an organization, such as a group practice, clinic, hospital, or other healthcare organization.

Make sure you are reviewing the correct individual or organization record.
Step 2 • Check Details

What Should You Review?

Legal name and credentials or suffixes, such as MD, PA, or RN.

NPI status, mailing address, primary practice address, and any secondary locations.

Other identifiers listed, such as a Medicare or Medicaid ID, when applicable.

Check every field for accuracy and make sure it reflects your current information.
Step 3 • Taxonomy

Check Your Primary Taxonomy

Your primary taxonomy should match the provider type you primarily practice as.

If your role, credentials, specialty, or practice focus has changed, review whether your taxonomy selection is still appropriate.

Taxonomy describes your provider type and specialty, not simply every credential you hold.
Step 4 • Licenses

Review State Information

Check the state or states where you practice and the corresponding state license number or numbers shown.

Confirm the details are current and consistent with your professional records and enrollment applications.

NPPES updates do not automatically update every payer or enrollment system.
Need to Correct?

Update Through NPPES

Go to NPPES.cms.hhs.gov and log in to your account.

Search using your NPI number, select the correct record, and make the necessary corrections.

Review your changes carefully and submit the update.
